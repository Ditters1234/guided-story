## Chapter development — implemented

- New stories default to 800 words per generated passage and an approximate 3,000-word chapter. Both targets are editable in setup and Settings → Story. Existing passage targets remain as saved.
- Reader and Contents show actual passage/chapter counts, word totals and completion state. “Begin chapter one” generates its first installment; continuation builds the chapter.
- The narrator receives the words already written in the current chapter and a chapter pacing brief. Automatic chapter endings are held until at least 80% of the target; a natural close can occur later. The reader can explicitly keep the chapter open or bring the next passage to a chapter ending, including a shorter chapter.
- A response below 60% of the passage target gets one bounded expansion pass using the original draft and context. If it still falls short, generation fails without committing prose, effects or memory. This is an approximate length floor, not a literary-quality evaluator. Context/output budgets and cancellation still apply. Dev retains the expansion request and length-check stages.
- Replacement counts only the surviving passages in that chapter. Starting a new chapter resets its word total. The short sample is not rewritten automatically.
- Continuity extraction fits its output allowance to the available budget, retaining full canon/prose and a minimum useful response reserve. Longer prose no longer fails solely because extraction unnecessarily reserves the narrator's full output-token ceiling.
- Evidenced item name/description/condition updates now have an explicit operation, with quantity/ownership/equipped changes kept separate and latest-passage replacement restoring the previous description.
